Frequently asked questions

Blurit / FAQ

What is Blurit?
An anonymous, text-only public board. There are no accounts, names, likes or replies. Posts appear in one shared feed and are deleted after 48 hours.
Do I need an account to post?
No. There is no sign-up and no login. You open the feed, write, and post.
Is Blurit really anonymous?
Posts are never linked to a name, account or profile, and Blurit's code never writes your IP address to its database. The server holds IP addresses briefly in memory only to stop spam, such as limiting how fast someone can post. Like any website, the hosting provider may keep its own network logs. See the privacy page for the full details.
Why do posts disappear after 48 hours?
So nothing you say follows you around. After 48 hours a post is removed from the feed, and expired posts are deleted from the database every hour.
Can I delete my post?
No. Since posts are not tied to anyone, there is no way to prove a post is yours. It stays until it expires or is removed through moderation. Think before you post.
Why can't I post links?
Links attract spam, scams and malware. Blurit is for words only.
How long can a post be?
Up to 500 characters of plain text.
Why are there no likes or replies?
Likes and replies turn posting into a performance. Without them there is nothing to chase and nobody to pile on.
How does reporting work?
Every post has a report link. Once a post gets enough reports it is hidden automatically. Illegal content can also be removed immediately by the site operator. Read the community rules.
Is Blurit open source?
Yes. The full code is on GitHub under the MIT license.
