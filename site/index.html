Blurit

Blurit is an anonymous, text-only public board where anyone can say what's on their mind. There are no accounts, no names and no login. You write, you post, and 48 hours later it is gone. Read the FAQ, the community rules and how we handle your privacy.

How it works

Write
Plain text only, up to 500 characters. Links are not allowed.
Post
Your post appears in one shared public feed straight away. It is not tied to any name, account or profile.
Gone
After 48 hours the post is removed from the feed and deleted from the database.

There are no likes, no replies and no follower counts. Nothing to build, nothing to keep score of. Just one shared feed that anyone can read.
