How to post anonymously online without an account

Blurit / Blog / 2 October 2026

Sometimes you want to say something without it being attached to you. Not to hurt anyone, just to get it out. But most places that call themselves anonymous still ask for an email address, a phone number or a social login first. That isn't anonymous. It is a username hiding a real identity that the company already has.

What to check before you trust an "anonymous" site

Does it need an account?
If you have to sign up, your posts can be linked back to whatever you signed up with.
Does it keep your posts forever?
Something you write today can be read years from now. Posts that expire carry far less risk.
Does it track you?
Cookies, analytics and ad scripts can follow you between sites even when you never give your name.
Can you read the code?
A privacy policy is a promise. Open source code lets anyone check whether the promise is kept.

How Blurit handles it

Blurit has no accounts at all. You open the feed, write up to 500 characters, and post. The post is not linked to any name or profile, the feed sets no cookies, and every post is deleted after 48 hours. The privacy page lists exactly what the server keeps, and the source code is public.

One honest limit

No website can promise perfect anonymity. Your internet provider and the site's hosting provider can see that a connection happened. If you need stronger protection, use a privacy-focused browser or Tor. And remember: what you write can identify you too, so leave out names and details.
