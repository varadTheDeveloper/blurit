Blurit / Rules

Blurit is anonymous, but it is not lawless. Posts are public and are not reviewed before they appear, so the community keeps the feed clean.

Not allowed

Illegal content
Anything illegal, including any sexual content involving minors. This is removed immediately and may be reported to authorities.
Threats and violence
Threatening a person or group, or encouraging violence.
Harassment
Targeting, bullying or abusing a specific person.
Personal information
Posting someone else's name, phone number, address or other private details.
Spam
Ads, repeated posts, or attempts to get around the no-links rule.

Reporting

Every post has a report link. Once a post gets enough reports it is hidden automatically.
